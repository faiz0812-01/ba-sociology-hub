Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This notice explains what information is handled when you use Academic Hub and which services help operate the site.

Browsing and traffic information

Academic Hub is hosted and operated with Cloudflare services. To deliver the site and understand its use, the site’s restricted owner traffic dashboard stores request details: your IP address, the page path, and approximate country, region, and city. When you move between sections, the page path and section name are also recorded. These traffic records are retained for up to seven days.

Google Analytics is used to understand how visitors use the site. Google may process usage and device information, including page views and interactions, through cookies or similar technologies. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Analytics privacy information.

Community chat

If you post in the community chat, the name you enter, your message, and its timestamp are stored by the site and shown publicly to visitors. The chat keeps up to 100 recent messages and removes messages after seven days. Do not post passwords, contact details, or other information you want to keep private.

AI Study Helper

If you submit a question to the optional AI Study Helper, your question, chosen subject and language, and up to four recent conversation messages are sent to Cloudflare’s AI service to generate a response. Academic Hub does not store the question-and-answer conversation in its own backend. A one-way daily hash of the visitor IP address is used by the backend to apply usage limits. The conversation is kept in your browser tab and clears when you reload or leave the page. Avoid including personal or confidential information. Cloudflare processes requests under its own Privacy Policy.

Advertising and cookies

If Google AdSense or other ads are shown on this site, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads. These technologies may be used to show ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites.

Learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services. You can manage personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings or visit aboutads.info choices.

Links to other services

Academic Hub may link to third-party services such as WhatsApp. If you choose to use an external service, information you provide there is handled under that service’s privacy terms. For WhatsApp, see its Privacy Policy.

Questions

For a privacy question about Academic Hub, please use the contact option on the home page.

Changes to this notice

This page may be updated when the site’s features or data practices change. The date at the top indicates the latest update.
