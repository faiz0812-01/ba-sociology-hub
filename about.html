About this learning library

About Academic Hub

Academic Hub brings study materials and exam practice together in one place for students working through university subjects.

What you’ll find here

The library is organized around BA Sociology and History study needs for Calicut University, with resources across subjects such as Sociology, English, Malayalam, History, Economics, Philosophy, Ethics, Mathematics, and Arabic. Depending on the subject, you can browse module and chapter resources, open PDFs, use bilingual study guides, or try a practice test.

How to use the resources

Choose a subject from the learning library, then select a module or chapter to find its available material. Practice tests are provided as revision exercises. The optional AI Study Helper can explain general concepts and suggest practice questions; it does not read the site’s PDFs or verify a course syllabus.

Study materials on this site are supplementary. Check your current syllabus, course announcements, and instructor guidance for official requirements. If you notice a broken link or an error in a resource, please use the contact option on the home page.
